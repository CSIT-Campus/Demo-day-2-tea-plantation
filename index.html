Our story

Mistyhill Ceylon Tea began in 1962, when D.M. Herath, a tea maker on a British-run estate, bought a small 10-acre plot in the hills of Nuwara Eliya.

What started as a family dream grew into a 150-acre estate. Today, his grandson Asanka Herath leads the business as the third generation, keeping the same promise: honest, hand-picked Ceylon tea.

  1. 1962 10 acres, bought by D.M. Herath
  2. Today 150 acres, run by the third generation
